# Sound credits

Everything under `cc0/` is dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not required;
it is given here gladly. `tools/fetch_audio.py` fetches these files again
from the recipe in `sources.json` and checks the downloads against
`audio.lock.json`; `manifest.json` says which file plays for which cue.

| Where | What | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cc0/kenney/impact/` | Impact Sounds: footsteps (stone, wood, grass, snow, carpet), impacts of flesh, wood, metal, glass and stone, bells | Kenney | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds |
| `cc0/kenney/rpg/` | RPG Audio: doors, coins, books, cloth and leather, blades, a creaking board, footsteps | Kenney | https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio |
| `cc0/kenney/interface/` | Interface Sounds: clicks, ticks, panels opening and closing | Kenney | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds |
| `cc0/kenney/jingles/` | Music Jingles: a steel jingle (level up), a pizzicato one (the hero's death) | Kenney | https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles |

These are placeholders, chosen by name and loudness, until better sounds
are picked.
